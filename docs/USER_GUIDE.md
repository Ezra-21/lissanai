# LissanAI User Guide

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. Visit the app and click **Get Started Free**
2. Fill in your name, email, and a strong password
3. Complete the short onboarding quiz to personalize your plan

### Setting Your Goals
Choose your primary focus during onboarding:
- **Job Interview Prep** – Mock interviews with AI feedback
- **Professional Writing** – Email drafting and grammar
- **Speaking Confidence** – Pronunciation and fluency
- **General English** – Balanced across all skills

## Features

### Mock Interviews
Select a job category (e.g., Software Developer) and start a session.
The AI asks one question at a time. After each answer you receive:
feedback on content, grammar, and delivery. Sessions are saved to history.

### Grammar Check
Paste or type any text (10–5,000 characters).
Get instant corrections, a 0-100 clarity score, and professional style tips.

### Email Drafting
Write rough notes in Amharic or English.
AI generates a polished, professional email using your chosen template.

### Pronunciation Coach
Record yourself reading a practice sentence.
See word-by-word accuracy scores and specific phoneme tips.

### Learning Path
Follow structured lessons from beginner to advanced.
Complete quizzes, track progress, and earn achievement badges.

## Tips for Success
- Use LissanAI for 15+ minutes daily to build a streak
- Review your AI-generated weekly study plan every Monday
- Check your progress heatmap each Sunday to spot gaps
